# Chosen Direction: Covenant Ledger

## Design Movement
**Contemporary Editorial Modernism** informed by premium annual reports, architectural journals, and mission-led philanthropic campaigns. The page should feel measured and sincere rather than promotional or ornamental.

## Core Principles
1. **Stewardship made visible:** Use structured information, transparent claims, and carefully staged outcomes to make the partnership understandable.
2. **Warm authority:** Pair the permanence of deep ink and charcoal with a restrained rust-red accent that communicates both conviction and care.
3. **Asymmetric composition:** Offset visual anchors and text blocks across an intentionally spacious editorial canvas instead of centering each section in uniform cards.
4. **Purpose before persuasion:** Make the ministry's 501(c)(3) standing, mission, and tangible outcomes clear before describing partnership options.

## Color Philosophy
The page centers a **parchment ground** (#F2F0E9) to evoke reflection, records, and stewardship. **Ink** (#1A1A1A) carries authority and readable contrast; **slate** (#2E4057) establishes trust for financial information; **covenant rust** (#8C2F00) is reserved for calls to action, key facts, and directional marks. The palette is calm, serious, and hospitable.

## Layout Paradigm
The experience unfolds as an **editorial dossier**: an offset hero, a wide mission declaration, a linear narrative of proof and impact, and a final invitation. Strong dividing rules, oversized pull quotes, and numbered sections create rhythm. On small screens, the same story collapses into a clear vertical reading order without losing hierarchy.

## Signature Elements
1. **Covenant rule:** A narrow rust-red line that marks headings, calls to action, and key transitions.
2. **Ledger numerals:** Large, restrained serif numbers that label models, outcomes, and process steps.
3. **Impact margin notes:** Small uppercase labels and citations that frame supporting context like a publication.

## Interaction Philosophy
Navigation should be purposeful and restrained. Section links smoothly guide visitors through the story; a clear invitation button scrolls to the partnership pathway. Content reveals should remain subtle and never interfere with reading or keyboard navigation.

## Animation
Use only brief opacity-and-translate entrance motion for major sections, with 50–70 ms staggered delays and a custom ease-out. Buttons may use a 160 ms color and 0.97 scale active response. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by rendering all content immediately with no motion.

## Typography System
**Cormorant Garamond** serves as the expressive editorial display face for headings, pull quotes, and large numerals. **Source Sans 3** carries body copy, labels, navigation, and metrics for clarity. Headlines are left-aligned, generous in leading, and never set in all caps; labels may use small, spaced uppercase Source Sans 3.

## Brand Essence
**LIMinistries helps real estate and lending professionals turn marketplace abundance into accountable, global Gospel-centered restoration.**

**Personality:** Stewardly, courageous, compassionate.

## Brand Voice
Headlines are invitational and decisive; CTAs are concrete and service-oriented; microcopy is transparent about process and impact. Avoid generic welcome language or exaggerated promises.

**Example lines:**
- “Let every closing carry a measure of restoration.”
- “See where generosity goes—and whom it helps.”

## Wordmark & Logo
Use a **covenant arch and rising path** symbol: two simple, interlocking geometric strokes that suggest shelter, a doorway, and a life moving forward. The mark appears without text in the header and favicon; the LIMinistries name is composed separately in the display typeface.

## Signature Brand Color
**Covenant Rust — #8C2F00**

## Style Decisions
- Build a presentation-inspired page, not a generic nonprofit template.
- Use real estate, partnership, prayer, and digital-ministry visuals only in the roles they play in the presentation.
- Keep financial and tax language informational; do not present it as individualized tax or legal advice.
- Repeat the covenant arch/rising-path mark as a quiet signature inside editorial section labels, rather than using generic decoration.
- Favor asymmetric dossier rows, margin numerals, pull facts, and strong dividing rules over repeated card-grid treatments in the middle of the page.
- Use documentary-feeling partnership and ministry imagery whenever possible; photography must support the real estate, prayer, media, and stewardship narrative rather than serve as generic lifestyle filler.
